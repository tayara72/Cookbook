Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cooksie handles information when you use the app to save, organize, import, back up, and restore recipes.

Information You Provide

Cooksie allows you to create and store recipes, including recipe names, ingredients, instructions, favorite selections, and other recipe content you choose to save.

When you use an import feature, you may provide content such as a recipe webpage URL, pasted text, or a selected image containing recipe information.

Recipe Import Processing

When you choose to import a recipe from a URL, pasted text, or photo, the content you submit may be transmitted to external processing services used by Cooksie to identify and organize recipe details such as the recipe title, ingredients, and instructions.

This processing occurs only when you choose to use an import feature. You can also enter recipes manually without submitting import content for this type of processing.

Recipe Storage and Backup

Saved recipes are stored in the app on your device.

Cooksie also allows you to create a JSON backup file of your recipe collection. When you choose to save a backup through the iOS Files sheet, you decide where that file is stored, which may include iCloud Drive or another storage location available on your device.

Restoring from a backup requires you to select a previously saved backup file.

Data We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Cooksie may process:

  • Recipe URLs you choose to import
  • Recipe text you paste into the app
  • Recipe images you select for import
  • Recipe information extracted from submitted content
  • Backup files you choose to export or restore

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the app's functions, including importing, formatting, displaying, saving, backing up, and restoring your recipes.

Your Choices

  • You can create recipes manually instead of using import features.
  • You can remove saved recipes from the app.
  • You control whether and where you export a backup file.

Children's Privacy

Cooksie is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app is intended for organizing recipe content provided by the user.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Cooksie's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
